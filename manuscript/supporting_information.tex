\section*{Overview}

This Supplementary Material collects the numerical diagnostics behind the structural assignment in the main text. The purpose is to separate three levels of evidence: (i) high-quality phenomenological reproduction of the measured complex \ce{SiO2}/water spectrum, (ii) transfer of the species-resolved \waterair fingerprints into selected spectral windows, and (iii) model selection among layer-populated water structures. The reference curve for the structural fits is a 17-point moving-average-smoothed version of the digitized experimental imaginary spectrum, stored as \path{imag_smooth} or \path{imag_exp_smooth}; it is not the fitted phenomenological resonance curve \path{imag_fit}. All water-structure fits use nonnegative weights, a floating linear baseline, a global frequency shift, and a global sign convention. The sign is bookkeeping for comparing digitized fingerprints; the structural interpretation follows the experimental convention used in the main text, where positive \imchi corresponds to OH/H orientation toward \ce{SiO2}.

\section*{Clean-Window Model Alternatives}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Direct comparison of the near-degenerate \SIrange{3400}{3800}{cm^{-1}} clean-window fits.}
\begin{tabular}{llcccp{4.0cm}}
\toprule
model & active motifs & $R^2$ & SSE & shift / \si{cm^{-1}} & structural limitation \\
\midrule
minimal & I,VIII & 0.9703 & 0.000997 & -28 & L2 bridge layer absent \\
preferred & I,II,VIII & 0.9720 & 0.000942 & -25 & three-motif L1--L2--L3 topology \\
unconstrained & I,II & 0.9707 & 0.000984 & -25 & deeper H-bonded L3 layer absent \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The preferred I+II+VIII model is therefore not a unique algebraic decomposition of the spectrum. It is preferred because it is the best clean-window three-motif layered model and because it preserves a hydrogen-bonded L1--L2--L3 topology. The incremental gain relative to I+VIII is modest ($\Delta R^2=0.00163$, $\Delta\mathrm{SSE}=5.49\times10^{-5}$) but statistically resolved in an approximate nested F test ($F=25.5$, $p=6.5\times10^{-7}$, one added structural weight). The F test is diagnostic rather than strict because the nonnegative weights and global frequency shift are reoptimized.
